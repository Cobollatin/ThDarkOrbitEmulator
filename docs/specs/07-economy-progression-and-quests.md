# SPEC-07E: Economy, progression, Skylab, and Galaxy Gates audit

This topical contract supplements `07-scalable-server-mesh-and-aspire.md`. Its number is a task reference, not the master `SPEC-07` mesh contract. These local menu rules are emulator defined; captured official post-company menu contracts are still unavailable.

## Current contracts

- `EconomyStore` owns account balances, ledger, inventory, shop purchases, auction bids and claims, recipe crafts, rewards, collector progress, and gate parts. Account advisory locks and transaction-scoped idempotency protect cross-replica mutations. Catalog and admin endpoints live in `Features/Economy/`; menu adapters live in `Features/IndexInternal/InternalStart/`.
- Skylab has three collectors: prometium, endurium, and terbium. Each begins at level 1 with 500 stock. Upgrade costs `100 × current level` of its own resource and stops at level 10. Whole elapsed minutes generate `10 × level` per collector per minute, at most 60 minutes per collection. A collection advances the saved time by all elapsed whole minutes, discarding production beyond the 60-minute window. A stale expected time, replayed key, insufficient balance, or collection before one minute produces no partial credit.
- Galaxy Gates spend one energy per part. One spin form admits 1–10 parts, up to the remaining requirement. Activation requires all parts and costs no energy. An activated gate rejects further spins or activation. Parts and energy survive cache loss.
- Quests, battle pass, payment, vouchers, and expeditions use account-scoped claim keys and durable reward ledger entries where the matching menu is implemented.

## Verification and remaining contracts

`LocalSkylabPostgresTests` covers upgrade, maximum level, elapsed minute boundaries, cap, stale forms, and cache loss. `LocalGalaxyGatePostgresTests` covers energy debit, spin replay, incomplete/full/activated gates, concurrency, and cache loss. `EconomyPostgresTests` covers durable balances and cross-replica mutation.

Task 6 adds durable progression in migration `031_skylab_jobs_and_gate_waves`: separate collector upgrade jobs, one refinery job per account, and gate runs. A timed collector upgrade reserves `100 × current level` of its own resource, takes `current level` whole minutes, and raises the level only on a ready claim. A refinery batch reserves 10 prometium and 10 endurium, takes five minutes, and credits 10 prometid on claim. PostgreSQL account locks, action keys, expected levels/waves, and transaction-scoped debits protect concurrent and replayed mutations. Existing instant collector upgrade actions remain accepted for legacy menu compatibility, while the hosted menu offers timed upgrades.

Migration `033_gate_combat` replaces unverified menu progression. Existing gate counters are reset to wave zero because old wave actions supplied no combat evidence. Alpha, Beta, and Gamma use ten ordered NPC waves: Streuner, Lordakia, Mordon, Saimon, Devolarium, Kristallin, Sibelon, Sibelonit, Kristallon, Small Kubikon. Alpha counts are `20,20,20,20,20,40,20,80,16,20`; Beta differs at Mordon 40 and Sibelonit 40; Gamma differs at Saimon 40 and Sibelonit 40. Delta uses ten mixed waves with totals `30,12,30,13,24,16,20,11,18,3`, tracking the ordered NPC groups from the [official Delta Gate FAQ](https://board-en.darkorbit.com/threads/delta-gate-faq.840/). The menu's signed attack form records a server-calculated hit against the current wave NPC. Each hit deals 1,000 damage to the NPC and 20 retaliation damage to the ship; one NPC has 1,000 HP. The run has five lives and 1,000 ship HP. Destruction deducts one life, restores HP if any life remains, and retains cleared NPCs. Zero lives blocks attacks. Wave transition requires the full NPC kill count. Direct calls to the old `AdvanceGateWaveAsync` cannot progress a run. These combat numbers and the menu combat surface are local emulator rules; gate combat is not yet a native world map instance.

The tenth cleared wave atomically grants base completion rewards via the economy ledger: Alpha `20,000` Uridium and UCB-100, `100,000` honour, `4,000,000` experience; Beta `40,000`, `200,000`, `8,000,000`; Gamma `60,000`, `300,000`, `12,000,000`; Delta `45,000`, `225,000`, `9,000,000`. A durable `gate_achievements` row records the ring/achievement state once. Stale waves, replayed action keys, completed runs, and exhausted lives cannot award again. `LocalGalaxyGatePostgresTests` covers composition, attack replay, kill gating, life loss, exhaustion, and all four completion ledgers. Wave counts and base rewards follow the [official DarkOrbit Galaxy Gates FAQ](https://board-en.darkorbit.com/threads/galaxy-gates-new-rewards-faq.127345/); event bonuses and additional item drops are outside this local contract.
